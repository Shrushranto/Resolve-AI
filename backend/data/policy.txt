# Return & Refund Policy — SupportAI E-Commerce

## General Return Policy
Customers may return most items within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund.
Items must be unused, undamaged, and in their original packaging.
Proof of purchase (order ID) is required for all returns.

## Electronics
Electronics may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
The device must be in original condition with all accessories and packaging.
Devices showing signs of physical damage or liquid damage are not eligible for refund.
Software-related issues may be addressed via support before initiating a return.

## Clothing & Apparel
Clothing items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be unwashed, unworn, and have all original tags attached.
Final sale items marked as non-returnable cannot be refunded.

## Furniture & Large Items
Furniture returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery.
Assembly damage or customer-caused damage voids the return eligibility.
Pickup logistics for large items may take 3–5 business days after refund approval.

## Refund Amounts & Thresholds
Refunds up to ₹5,000 are processed automatically within 3–5 business days.
Refunds above ₹5,000 require review by our support team and may take 5–7 business days.
Refunds are credited to the original payment method.

## Non-Returnable Items
Perishable goods, digital downloads, and hygiene products are non-returnable.
Gift cards and store credit are non-refundable.
Customized or personalized items cannot be returned unless defective.

## Escalation & Human Review
Cases involving disputes, high-value refunds, or policy exceptions are reviewed by our support team.
You will receive an email notification once a decision is made.
To escalate a case, please describe your issue and a support agent will review within 24 hours.

## Duplicate Requests
If you have already submitted a refund request for an order, please do not submit again.
Duplicate submissions are automatically detected and will not create multiple refund records.
Contact support if you have not received a response within 7 business days.
